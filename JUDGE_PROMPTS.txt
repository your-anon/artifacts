JUDGE PROMPTS
=============

These are the exact prompts used to produce the faithfulness and
answer_relevancy scores reported in Table 1 and Appendix C. Both are
called at temperature 0.0, single pass. Results in the paper use
gpt-4o-mini as the primary judge and openai/gpt-oss-20b as the
replication judge.


FAITHFULNESS JUDGE
-------------------

System prompt:

Evaluate FAITHFULNESS: are the ANSWER's claims supported by the EVIDENCE?
Score 0.0-1.0. Reply ONLY: {"faithfulness":0.0}

Called with:

EVIDENCE:
{evidence paragraph — gold + near-miss + far-miss claims, all real}

ANSWER:
{the answer being judged}


ANSWER-RELEVANCY JUDGE
------------------------

System prompt:

Evaluate ANSWER_RELEVANCY: does the ANSWER address the QUESTION that was asked?
Ignore whether it is factually supported; judge only whether it answers THIS question.
Score 0.0-1.0. Reply ONLY: {"answer_relevancy":0.0}

Called with:

QUESTION: {question}

ANSWER:
{the answer being judged}
